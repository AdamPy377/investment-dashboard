Record dividend reinvestment

Record the dividend as income and the new shares as a purchase together. This adds no external deposit. Any unspent amount stays in a separate balance for this holding and broker and is used on the next reinvestment. Broker cash is unchanged. Shares cannot cost more than the dividend plus the carried balance.
